# Journey status

1. Call `list_organizations` and settle on the workspace, then `list_journeys`. If no journey was named, ask which one.
2. Call `get_journey`.
3. Reply stage by stage. For each step give its name, then the needs, pain points, opportunities and solutions on it, quoted exactly, with the highest-priority pain point first.
4. Close with three lines: steps with nothing on them, pain points without a linked answer, and the journey's Orchetta link.
5. Offer the `review-journey` skill for a full check. Don't change anything.
