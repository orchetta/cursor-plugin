# Top pain points

1. Call `list_organizations` and settle on the workspace.
2. Call `list_resource_kinds` to find the pain point kind and the fields that rank it (priority, severity or score).
3. Call `list_resources` for that kind and sort highest first by those fields.
4. Reply with the top ten: name quoted exactly, its ranking values, and the journey › stage › step it sits on, with its Orchetta link.
5. Note any pain point with no linked opportunity or solution. Offer the `add-insights` skill to answer it. Don't change anything.
